<h3>IIIF "Literacy"</h3>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
            <li><span style="font-size: 12pt;">the ability to read and write ✔</li>
            <li><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><span class="AraNOb">competence or knowledge in a specified area.</li>
</ol>

<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">

<h3>What Does IIIF Stand For?</h3>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
            <li><span style="font-size: 12pt;">High quality authentic cultural heritage content <em>as objects of inquiry</em></li>
            <li><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Global community of smart people from many fields with a shared interest</li>
            <li><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Empowered users and <em>re-</em>users</li>
            <li><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Nifty tools and gadgets, from DIY to DDIY</li>
            <li><span style="font-size: 12pt;">also: The <span style="text-decoration: underline;">International <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Image <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Interoperability <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Framework</li>
</ol>


<h3>Glosses</h3>
<ul>
<li><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Interoperability: the ability to share and access digital images, audio, and video in a standardized way </li>
</ul>
<p style="padding-left: 40px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><em>*a standardized method of describing and delivering images over the web, as well as "presentation based metadata" (that is, structural metadata) about structured sequences of images. If institutions holding artworks, books, newspapers, manuscripts, maps, scrolls, single sheet collections, and archival materials provide IIIF endpoints for their content, any IIIF-compliant viewer or application can consume and display both the images and their structural and presentation</em> metadata." (Thanks, <a class="inline_disabled" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Image_Interoperability_Framework" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Wikipedia</a>!<em>)</em></p>

</ul>
