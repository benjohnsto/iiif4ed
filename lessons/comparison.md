<p>Below are selected pages from two illuminated manuscripts: Ethiopic Folding Picture Book (left) and Homilies and Miracles of St. Michael (right). View the pages with zoom and explore the full codices, then use the cropping tool below to extract a detail you would like to share and discuss with the class.</p>

{%- include share.html -%}

<p>Look closely at the two illuminations for comparable details: what similarities and differences do you see? How might you interpret these relationships, given the different geographical, cultural, and historical settings? Working one at a time, copy the IIIF Manifest links below and paste them into the entry blank. Click Submit to load an item, zoom in to view details, then click to the pencil button to highlight details you would like to share. Crop as many as you like, and when you settle on one you would like to share, click its thumbnail below. The returns box offers several choices for linking media. Select HTML and Copy. For your responses, click reply, then find and click the &lt;/&gt; HTML button at the bottom right of your text editor. Paste the embed code, then click the &lt;/&gt; button again to see your image. Add comments and submit your reply!</p>
