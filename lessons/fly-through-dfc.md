{%- include heading name="activity" -%}


<p><img src="https://media.getty.edu/iiif/image/dfe34df8-66ae-4e08-82e6-60329a6385df/1872,1579,1617,841/full/0/default.jpg" alt="Illustration of a feather above the water's surface. It is a conceptual reference to the color deep green" width="100%" height="100%" /></p>
<p>The Digital Florentine Codex is an online "digital edition" of the <em>Florentine Codex</em> produced at the Getty Research Institute by a team of scholars, designers, and technologists (including myself and a former Oxy student!). We will explore this remarkable resource, and learn some of its sophisticated features for scholarly use. This Canvas discussion-based lab activity will support our active engagement with embedded tools and instructions.</p>
<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Objectives</strong></span></summary>
<ul>
<li>To familiarize you with the images as a &ldquo;third&rdquo; text of the DFC</li>
<li>To learn the conventions of Mesoamerican iconography</li>
<li>To learn how the folios are numbered and cited</li>
<li>To learn writing conventions when using images (captions, citation)</li>
</ul>
</details>

{%- include heading name="example" -%}


<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Feather / Ihhuitl</strong><br />Consider the example of The Feather in the Digital Florentine Codex&nbsp; <span style="color: #236fa1;"><em>(click to open and close)<br /></em></span></span></summary>
<p>As vocabulary in the digital edition, <span style="color: #000000;">"feather" and one of its Nahuatl translations "ihhuatl"</span>&nbsp;can be searched for either Text or Image returns. Depending upon which you select, nearly all books in the Florentine Codex are represented in the returns, and the variety of content associated with these search terms is astounding. It is easy to see, in this sense, how pervasive feathers are in Nahua history and culture. Here, the profession of feather seller as two types serves as a kind of metaphor for people in general.&nbsp;</p>
<p><a class="inline_disabled" href="https://florentinecodex.getty.edu/book/10/folio/44r" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>The feather seller</strong></a></p>
<p><img style="float: right;" src="https://media.getty.edu/iiif/image/85477637-2d57-412b-abdb-0590cb96b7aa/939,4228,1164,596/1400,/0/default.jpg" alt="Image of a feather seller from the Florentine Codex: a man sitting on the ground with a basket and one feather" width="50%" height="50%" />The feather seller is a feature worker, a merchant-the man [with] the basket.</p>
<p>The good feather seller [is] a gentle worker-one who esteems [his wares], who is dedicated. He sells various feathers-precious feathers; he sells fine green feathers, chili-green feathers, this curved at the tip, the feathers of young birds. He sells [feathers of the trogonorous, the troupial, the blue cotinga.</p>
<p>The bad feather worker [is] a [fraudulent] embellisher of feathers, a treater of feathers with glue. He sells old, worn feathers, damaged feathers. He dyes feathers; he dyes those which are faded, dirty, yellow, darkened, smoked.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>

{%- include heading name="tools" -%}


<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Mirador Viewer</strong><br />The cartoon is pre-loaded in this activity, but here are general instructions for comparative viewing &nbsp;<span style="color: #236fa1;"><em>(click to open and close)</em></span></span></summary>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>Copy a IIIF manifest URI</li>
<li>Click the <strong>+START HERE</strong> button in the upper left</li>
<li>Then click the <strong>+ADD RESOURCE </strong>button on the lower right (the page may jump down a bit)</li>
<li>Paste the manifest URI in the <strong>Resource location </strong>blank and click&nbsp;<strong>ADD</strong>. The item should load and display an item bar</li>
<li>Click the item bar to load the item in the viewer</li>
<li>For comparative viewing, repeat this process with another resource. You can do this multiple times</li>
<li>Item windows can be dragged into different configurations using their header bar</li>
<li>Zoom in and out, click through pages, and acclimate yourself to the many viewing settings, including thumbnail, single page, etc.</li>
</ol>
<iframe src="https://mcgrawcenter.github.io/mirador/?manifest=https://media.getty.edu/iiif/manifest/d8a35385-d097-4306-89c0-1a15aa74e6da" width="100%" height="700" loading="lazy"></iframe>
</details>
<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>IIIF Cropping Tool</strong><br />Use this tool to find the URI for an image in a IIIF manifest, and to crop a detail from that image&nbsp; <span style="color: #236fa1;"><em>(click to open and close)</em></span></span></summary>
<h4>Instructions</h4>
<ol>
<li>Click the page thumbnail to load in the viewer</li>
<li>Zoom and view the item&nbsp;</li>
<li>To crop a detail from the image, click the white pencil icon, and highlight the desired portion by clicking and dragging a rectangle from corner to corner</li>
<li>A thumbnail of your selected detail will appear in a slider at the bottom of the Cropping Tool. You can repeat to collect multiple details.</li>
<li>Click on the thumbnail of any detail to generate an image URL.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Click Copy to copy the Image URL</li>
<li><strong>If you would like the URL for the full page</strong>, click the page thumbnail, and Copy the URL</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip</strong>: make sure to select <strong>Full</strong> size before you Copy the URL!</p>
<iframe src="https://mcgrawcenter.github.io/croppingtool/?manifest=https://media.getty.edu/iiif/manifest/d8a35385-d097-4306-89c0-1a15aa74e6da" width="100%" height="700" loading="lazy"></iframe>
