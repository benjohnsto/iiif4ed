## {{page.title}}

{%- include heading name="activity" -%}

<p>This activity consists of two part: 3D scanning of an object (one of the five collected items pertaining to the Botanical Garden plant you investigated) and annotating in Sketchfab.</p>


{%- include heading name="tools" -%}


<p>Download <a class="inline_disabled external fOyUs_bGBk fbyHH_bGBk fbyHH_vIby" href="https://poly.cam/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span>Polycam application</span><span><span class="screenreader-only">Links to an external site.</span></span></a> into your device (e.g. phone).</p>


{%- include heading name="steps" -%}


<ol>
<li>Following the <a class="inline_disabled external fOyUs_bGBk fbyHH_bGBk fbyHH_vIby" href="https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/11EPGcB3bT5RKkN5D8E-zC4-e3jU0f0P9K9CgQFQog7k/edit#slide=id.g16357c8ed04_0_12" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span>Polycam tutorial</span></a> to scan of an object from one of the five items you collected from the UCLA Botanical Garden.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Once scanned, open your cature in Polycam on your device.</li>
<li>Export to GLTF.</li>
<li>Email/Airdrop to your computer.&nbsp;</li>
<li class="li1">Open your computer to access the file.</li>
<li class="li1">Go to the Sketchfab website, create an account by linking to your google account.</li>
<li class="li1">Click on &ldquo;Upload&rdquo; (orange rectangular button on the upper right corner).</li>
<li class="li1">Drag and drop the file before uploading it.</li>
<li class="li1">Enter &ldquo;Title&rdquo; and &ldquo;Description&rdquo; of the model.</li>
<li class="li1">Click on &ldquo;Edit 3D Settings.&rdquo;</li>
<li class="li1">Click on &ldquo;Annotation&rdquo; icon (in the shape of a raindrop).</li>
<li class="li1">Put it on the location in the model where you will annotate.</li>
<li class="li1">Write down the title, description (where you describe and interpret the part), before clicking on &ldquo;okay.&rdquo;</li>
<li class="li1">Repeat 11-13 to have 3 annotations on this 3D image.</li>
<li class="li1">Click on &ldquo;Save Settings&rdquo; on the upper right corner of the page, before clicking on &ldquo;Exit.&rdquo;</li>
<li class="li1">Click on &ldquo;Publish&rdquo; on the upper right corner, before clicking &ldquo;View My Model.&rdquo;</li>
<li class="li1">Click on &lt;/&gt; at the bottom right corner outside of the text editor in Canvas.</li>
