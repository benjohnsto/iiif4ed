{%- include heading name="steps" -%}
<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Part 1:</strong> Solve the tile puzzle above&nbsp;<span style="color: #236fa1;"><em><br /></em></span></span></summary>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>Look closely at image details in discrete tiles to see what they depict, and how they align</li>
<li>Click any tile to move it to the one empty space</li>
<li>Review and reflect upon the full image when you've completed the puzzle</li>
</ol>
</details>
<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Part 2: </strong>View and crop imagery in Mixtape<span style="color: #236fa1;"><em><br /></em></span></span></summary>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>Highlight any item in Mixtape</li>
<li>Select the Cropping icon <img src="https://cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com/512/2301/2301191.png" alt="crop icon" width="23" height="23" /></li>
<li>Click any page thumbnail to load in the <strong>IIIF Cropping Tool</strong> viewer</li>
<li>Zoom and view</li>
<li>To crop an image or other detail from the page, click the white pencil icon, and highlight the desired rectangle by clicking and dragging a rectangle from corner to corner</li>
<li>A thumbnail of your selected detail will appear in a slider at the bottom of the Cropping Tool. You can repeat to collect multiple details.</li>
<li>Click to highlight the detail thumbnail you would like to use</li>
<li>Copy the detail URL displayed in the box above on the upper left (make sure it copies)</li>
</ol>
</details>
<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Part 3: </strong>Create and share your own IIIF puzzle&nbsp;<span style="color: #236fa1;"><em><br /></em></span></span></summary>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>Open the <strong>Tile Puzzle Tool</strong></li>
<li>Paste your detail URL from the Cropping Tool into the box that says "Enter a IIIF image URL" and click <strong>Go</strong></li>
<li>Check and choose settings for number of tiles and size, and click <strong>Go</strong> again</li>
<li>Click on the puzzle link</li>
<li>Click Reply in the discussion forum below and follow the directions in the Share Section&nbsp;</li>
</ol>
</details>
</div>
