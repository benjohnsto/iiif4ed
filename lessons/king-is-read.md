{%- include heading name="steps" -%}


<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Individual v. multiple items</strong><br />Mixtape allows you to view, crop, and generate manifests for one item, selected items, or the whole collection, &nbsp;<span style="color: #236fa1;"><em>(click to open and close)</em></span></span></summary>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>Under each item thumbnail are icons for Mirador (viewer), Cropping Tool, IIIF (manifest), and a check box. Use any of these for a single item</li>
<li>To use multiple select items, check their boxes and click the desired function from the icons in the upper right corner</li>
<li>To use the whole collection, leave the boxes unchecked and click any of the icons in the upper right corner</li>
</ol>
</details>
<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Viewing in Mirador</strong><br />To load an item for viewing in deep zoom. <span style="color: #236fa1;"><em>(click to open and close)</em></span></span></summary>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>Click the <strong>M</strong> icon in the thumbnail box to load Mirador</li>
<li>For comparative viewing, check the boxes for items you would like to see, and click the M icon in the upper right corner</li>
<li>Click the <strong>+START HERE</strong> button in the upper left</li>
<li>Then click the <strong>+ADD RESOURCE </strong>button on the lower right (the page may jump down a bit)</li>
<li>Paste the manifest URI in the <strong>Resource location </strong>blank and click&nbsp;<strong>ADD</strong>. The item should load and display an item bar</li>
<li>Click the item bar to load the item in the viewer</li>
<li>Repeat for additional items</li>
<li>Item windows can be dragged into different configurations using their header bar</li>
<li>Zoom in and out, click through pages, and acclimate yourself to the many viewing settings, including thumbnail, single page, etc.</li>
</ol>
</details>
<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Cropping a IIIF image</strong><br />To select a portion of an image <span style="color: #236fa1;"><em>(click to open and close)</em></span></span></summary>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>Select the Cropping icon <img src="https://cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com/512/2301/2301191.png" alt="crop icon" width="23" height="23" />in an item thumbnail</li>
<li>Click thumbnail to load in the <strong>IIIF Cropping Tool</strong> viewer</li>
<li>Zoom and view</li>
<li>To crop a detail from the image, click the white pencil icon, and highlight the desired rectangle by clicking and dragging a rectangle from corner to corner</li>
<li>A thumbnail of your selected detail will appear in a slider at the bottom of the Cropping Tool. You can repeat to collect multiple details.</li>
<li>Click the Copy icon <img src="https://static.thenounproject.com/png/361082-200.png" alt="Copy icon" width="24" height="24" /> on the left side of the thumbnail</li>
</ol>
</details>
<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Posting with an image</strong><br />To add image details to a discussion post <span style="color: #236fa1;"><em>(click to open and close)</em></span></span></summary>
<ol>
<li>Click Reply</li>
<li>To enter your image details, simply paste into the text field</li>
<li>Images can be manually resized by highlighting and dragging their corners</li>
<li>Repeat for additional images</li>
</ol>
</details>
