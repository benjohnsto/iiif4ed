{%- include heading name="steps" -%}


<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>Complete the form (shown in Tool).&nbsp;</li>
<li>Type in a provisional title for your narrative.</li>
<li>Add your name(s) as Author, as follows: Firstname Lastname.</li>
<li>Enter a description, which should make reference to a project idea, and provide information about the source, as the main thesis of a perceivable sequential narrative.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Allow Duplication.</strong></li>
<li>Prove you are not a robot, and read and accept the Terms of Service.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Create Exhibit.</strong></li>
<li>Write down the URL of your <strong>Exhibit</strong>, where you can return to do edits if necessary.</li>
<li>Click "Add Item."</li>
<li>Copy each IIIF Manifest URL then paste it in the blank before clicking "Import." <strong></strong></li>
<li>Repeat until you have imported all the items.</li>
<li>Choose one item to start, and click "Add to exhibit."</li>
<li>Click "+" on the left side of the page, zoom in or out of the image on the right, and lock the detail (image) you would like to do the caption on.</li>
<li>Write the caption (description and/or significance of the image) on the left side where it shows "Enter your comment here."</li>
<li>Once you are satisfied with the detail of the image and the caption, click on the check icon.</li>
<li>Use advance arrows change pages of the same item.</li>
<li>When the first item is done, go back to the list and select the second item and repeat the process.</li>
<li>You will compose a perceivable sequential narrative that connects captions and views like beads on a string.</li>
</ol>
