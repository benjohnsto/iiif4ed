## Map Georeferencing Lab Activity
<p>This lab activity will lead you through the process of geo-referencing a map from Princeton Library's Maps and Geospatial Center using the AllMaps tool.</p>
<p>An example of a finished, geo-referenced map is below:</p>

<h3>Create your own geo-referenced map</h3>
<p>Go to the <a class="inline_disabled" href="https://allmaps.org/#getting-started" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Getting Started section</a> of the Allmaps.org website.&nbsp; From there you can find a list of links to institutions that offer maps in the IIIF format:</p>
<h3>Using Princeton's collection</h3>
<ul>
    <li>Go to the <a class="inline_disabled" href="https://maps.princeton.edu/?utf8=%E2%9C%93&amp;search_field=all_fields&amp;q=" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Digital Maps &amp; Geospatial Data portal</a>.</li>
    <li>Set the <strong>Institution</strong> filter to 'Princeton' and set <strong>Access</strong> to 'public'</li>
    <li>Search for a map you would like to geo-reference and click the link to open the map on the Princeton library site.</li>
    <li>In the bottom-left corner of the map viewer, click the IIIF logo to open a new browser tab with a <em>manifest</em>, containing information about this map.&nbsp; This is the file that is used by AllMaps to perform geo-location.</li>
    <li><a href="https://editor.allmaps.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the AllMaps Editor</a> and paste in the URL of the manifest.</li>
</ul>
