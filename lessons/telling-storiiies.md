{%- include heading name="custom" text="✉️ Share (Creating an assignment)" -%}

<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">You can use this lab activity as a template to create your own collective Storiiies by changing two links, one in the "View" section and the other in the "Edit" section. Follow the steps below for these two links:</span></p>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>Go to the Module of "Hands-On Digital" to duplicate this lab activity by clicking the three dots on the right side and select duplicate. You can rename your copied lab activity.</li>
<li>Complete the form in the <a class="inline_disabled" href="https://storiiies-editor.cogapp.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Storiiies editor</a>.</li>
<li><span>Insert IIIF manifest or info.json URL of the image that you'd like to use.</span></li>
<li>Type in a provisional title of your Storiiie, which should somehow make reference to your research topic and the significance of the plant.</li>
<li>Type in your names in the Author section.</li>
<li>Type in your email address (to receive a confirmation of creating a Storiiies).</li>
<li>Enter a brief description of this narrative.</li>
<li>Click "Edit story" to bring up the image and begin viewing.</li>
<li>Click "Links"<strong> </strong>to get the <strong>View Link</strong> and the <strong>Edit Link</strong>.</li>
<li>In the html editor, replace the&nbsp;<strong>View Link</strong> and the <strong>Edit Link</strong>.</li>
</ol>
