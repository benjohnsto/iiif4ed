<h3>IIIF "Literacy"</h3>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
  <li>the ability to read and write ✔</span></li>
  <li><span>competence</span> or knowledge in a specified area.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Indicators of IIIF Literacy</h3>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
  <li>proper pronunciation of the term "iiif"</li>
  <li>a happy sensation seeing the <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e8/International_Image_Interoperability_Framework_logo.png" alt="IIIF Logo" width="31" height="31" /> logo</li>
</ol>

<h3>Proper Pronunciation of "iiif"</h3>
<ul>
  <li>"triple-eye eff"</span></li>
  <li><span style="text-decoration: line-through;">"eye triple eff"</span></li>
  <li><span style="text-decoration: line-through;">"eye, eye, eye, eff"</span></li>
  <li><span style="text-decoration: line-through;">"<strong>&iexcl;Ay, ay, ay eff!</strong>"</span></li>
</ul>

<h3>What Does IIIF Stand For?</h3>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
  <li>High quality authentic cultural heritage content <em>as objects of inquiry</em></span></li>
  <li>Global community of smart people from many fields with a shared interest</span></li>
  <li>Empowered users and <em>re-</em>users</span></li>
  <li>Nifty tools and gadgets, from DIY to DDIY</span></li>
  <li>also: The <span style="text-decoration: underline;">I</span>nternational <span style="text-decoration: underline;">I</span>mage <span style="text-decoration: underline;">I</span>nteroperability <span style="text-decoration: underline;">F</span>ramework</span></li>
</ol>
<h3>Glosses</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Interoperability: <span>the ability to share and access digital images, audio, and video in a standardized way </span></li>
</ul>
<p style="padding-left: 40px;"><em>*a standardized method of describing and delivering images over the web, as well as "presentation based metadata" (that is, structural metadata) about structured sequences of images. If institutions holding artworks, books, newspapers, manuscripts, maps, scrolls, single sheet collections, and archival materials provide IIIF endpoints for their content, any IIIF-compliant viewer or application can consume and display both the images and their structural and presentation</em> metadata." (Thanks, <a class="inline_disabled" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Image_Interoperability_Framework" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Wikipedia</a>!<em>)</em></p>

<ul>
  <li>OpenSeadragon: "an open-source, web-based viewer for <strong>high-resolution zoomable images</strong>, implemented in pure JavaScript, for desktop and mobile"</span></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="" style="background-color: #910000; color: #ffffff; padding: 0.2em 0.6em; border-radius: 30px;">👊 Punchline</h2>

<p>IIIF is an extension of writing into visuo-verbal and other sensory modalities to represent the ontological status of objects as information. Thus IIIF <em>literacy</em> is the ability to read and write with online digitized facsimiles of cultural heritage collections.</p>
