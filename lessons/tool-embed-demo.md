<h3 class="" style="background-color: #2774ae; color: #ffffff; border-color: #2774ae;">&nbsp;Steps</h3>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>Copy and paste the IIIF manifest URL of an item into the top bar and click SUBMIT</li>
<li>Select and click any thumbnail to load in the viewer</li>
<li>Zoom and view the item page&nbsp;</li>
<li>To crop a detail from the image, click the white pencil icon, and highlight the desired portion by clicking and dragging a rectangle from corner to corner (<strong>note:</strong> details are sized according to how large they appear in your viewer, so zoom in as much as you can for detail).</li>
<li>A thumbnail of your selected detail will appear in a slider at the bottom of the Cropping Tool. You can repeat to collect multiple details.</li>
<li>Click on the thumbnail of any detail to generate an image URL or HTML embed code, which you can choose, by clicking their respective buttons.</li>
<li>Click Copy to Copy the URL or embed code</li>
</ol>
</div>
