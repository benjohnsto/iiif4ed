## {{page.title}}


{%- include heading name="concept" -%}


<h4>Think Like a Curator</h4>
<p>A curator selects and interprets works for an exhibit.&nbsp; Curating a number of digital items (from the items in the <a class="inline_disabled" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13DvFQ2mgrz7ACoXpKpMql2k3N9KX8MosG9qrwcOXdIE/edit#gid=979331913" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Course Spreadsheet- in tab: "<span>Group and Info of the Selected Items"</span></a>) into your group project (a digital exhibit in an online platform) in a collaborative effort requires your collective conception of your project topic and the substances from selected items.</p>

{%- include heading name="activity" -%}


<p>Working with your group members, this activity is to utilize the method of comparative analysis for all of the selected items from all the group members (see them in the <a class="inline_disabled" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13DvFQ2mgrz7ACoXpKpMql2k3N9KX8MosG9qrwcOXdIE/edit#gid=979331913" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Course Spreadsheet- in tab: "<span>Group and Info of the Selected Items"</span></a>). The the group decides the topic and the title of the group project, and select curated items<a class="inline_disabled" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XEpCFOUFBlLbjGSkbdAbtGmtYO4pfWa38qfdrfL-sss/edit#gid=0" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> </a>into the <a class="inline_disabled" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13DvFQ2mgrz7ACoXpKpMql2k3N9KX8MosG9qrwcOXdIE/edit#gid=1711674363" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Course Spreadsheet - in tab: "Group Curated Items."</a></p>


{%- include heading name="materials" -%}



<p>Project digital items in the <a class="inline_disabled" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13DvFQ2mgrz7ACoXpKpMql2k3N9KX8MosG9qrwcOXdIE/edit#gid=979331913" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Course spreadsheet - in tab: "<span>Groups and Selected Items</span></a><a class="inline_disabled" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1EidTAhXurVT5sr0-MC7j2XEr4r_PKTGaMgCQmoF_cS0/edit#gid=1406012433" target="_blank" rel="noopener">."</a></p>


{%- include heading name="tools" -%}



<p>The <a class="inline_disabled" title="Link" href="https://etcpanel.princeton.edu/IIIF/mirador/v3anno/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Mirador</a> viewer.</p>

{%- include heading name="steps" -%}



<ol>
<li>Use Mirador for comparative analysis. First, open <a class="inline_disabled" title="Link" href="https://etcpanel.princeton.edu/IIIF/mirador/v3anno/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Mirador</a> viewer in another tab.</li>
<li>Click "+START HERE" (at the upper left corner), click " + ADD RESOURCES" (lower right corner), then put an item's IIIF Manifest URL in the Resource location, and "Add."</li>
<li>Repeat Step 2 until all the items are in the list.</li>
<li>Open items in the view and discuss their connections, differences, similarities.</li>
<li>Repeat Step 4 until you reach a consensus of an overarching theme that can thread through at least 6 items.</li>
<li>Write down the overarching theme as your Group Project Topic.</li>
<li>List all these 6 items' info in the Columns E-G in the <a class="inline_disabled" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13DvFQ2mgrz7ACoXpKpMql2k3N9KX8MosG9qrwcOXdIE/edit#gid=1711674363" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Course Spreadsheet</a>: item title, Page URL, III Image URL, IIIF Manifest URL, and the relevance to the overarching theme.</li>
<li>Decide a tentative topic and title for your Group Project in Columns C-D.</li>
</ol>
