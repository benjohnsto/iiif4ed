<details style="color: #000000;">
<summary><span style="color: #000000; font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Cropping a IIIF image</strong><br />To select a portion of an image <span style="color: #236fa1;"><em>(click to open and close)</em></span></span></summary>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Copy and paste a manifest URL into the Submit box</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Click any thumbnail to load in the <strong>IIIF Cropping Tool</strong> viewer</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Zoom and view</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;">To crop a detail from the image, click the white pencil icon, and highlight the desired rectangle by clicking and dragging a rectangle from corner to corner</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;">A thumbnail of your selected detail will appear in a slider at the bottom of the Cropping Tool. You can repeat to collect multiple details, from various manifests</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Click the Copy icon on the left side of the thumbnail</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Alternatively</strong>: any highlighted thumbnail (pages or cropped details) will display its image URL in full or actual size in the window with the Copy button. HTML embed code with manifest link is also available by clicking the &lt;/&gt; tab</span></li>
</ol>
</details>
