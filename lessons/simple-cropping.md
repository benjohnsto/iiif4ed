<p>Copy the IIIF Manifest link below and paste it into the entry blank. Click Submit to load the item, zoom in to view details, then click to the pencil button to highlight details you would like to share. Crop as many as you like, and when you settle on one you would like to share, click its thumbnail below. The returns box offers several choices for linking media. Select HTML and Copy. For your responses, click reply, then find and click the &lt;/&gt; HTML button at the bottom right of your text editor. Paste the embed code, then click the &lt;/&gt; button again to see your image. Add comments and submit your reply!</p>
