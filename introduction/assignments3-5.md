## {{page.title}}
<h3>Assignment 3 - Compare two manuscripts</h3>
<p>Compare two manuscript copies of <em>De natura rerum</em> by Thomas of Cantimpr&eacute;. Open both books on the folio with a miniature of a cyclops.</p>
<ul>
    <li>Brugge, Openbare Bibliotheek, Manuscript 411: <a href="https://sharedcanvas.be/IIIF/manifests/B_OB_MS411" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://sharedcanvas.be/IIIF/manifests/B_OB_MS411</a></li>
    <li>Gent, Sint-Baafskapittel, Manuscript 15: <a href="https://adore.ugent.be/IIIF/manifests/archive.ugent.be%3AB6F50BB0-7602-11EB-81AA-AFF9AA36FAF6" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://adore.ugent.be/IIIF/manifests/archive.ugent.be%3AB6F50BB0-7602-11EB-81AA-AFF9AA36FAF6</a></li>
</ul>
<h3>Assignment 4 - Create your own collage</h3>
<p>Find images of a specific iconographical topic using the <a href="https://portail.biblissima.fr/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Biblissima Portal</a>. Make a collage in Mirador with at least three images.</p>
<p>Tip: Take into account that the website's descriptions are written in French and/or English. Searching for terms like Apocalypse, dragon, Jerusalem&hellip; yields the best results for this assignment.</p>
<h3>Assignment 5 - Add items to an existing collage</h3>
<p>We have prepared collages on Behemoth, Boethius, Juvenal and cyclopes for you to play with.</p>
<ul>
    <li>Go to <a href="https://www.mmmonk.be/en/about-iiif/workshops/codes-mirador" target="_blank" rel="noopener">this page</a>,</li>
    <li>select a collage,</li>
    <li>import the code into a Mirador workspace,</li>
    <li>and start adding items to the collage.</li>
</ul>
