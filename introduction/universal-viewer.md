## {{page.title}}

<img src="{{site.baseurl}}/assets/images/uv.jpg"/>
<p>Universal Viewer is a IIIF-enabled viewer commonly used on Library and Museum websites.  You can likely used it to view digital collections. Universal Viewer, however, can also be used to view any IIIF resource.</p>
<h3>How to</h3>
<ol>
    <li>Select and copy the manifest URL of the object of your choice, for instance <a href="https://sharedcanvas.be/IIIF/manifests/B_GS_MS157-188" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://sharedcanvas.be/IIIF/m...</a>.</li>
    <li>Open the website <a href="https://universalviewer.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://universalviewer.io/</a></li>
    <li>Copy the URL to &lsquo;Paste the URL of a IIIF manifest here to see the UV in action!&rsquo;</li>
</ol>
<p>Imagine you have found an interesting detail in a manuscript and wish to share this with someone or to save it for yourself. Instead of downloading the image or taking a screen shot, you can share or bookmark a URL with coordinates using the Universal Viewer.</p>
<h3>How to</h3>
<ol>
    <li>Open a manifest in Universal Viewer, as above.</li>
    <li>Browse the manuscript and zoom in on an interesting detail. (Watch how the coordinates in the URL change as you zoom or move. The coordinates make it possible to share a specific area of an image.)</li>
    <li>Copy the URL with the coordinates or use the share button in the viewer. Share the URL in a message, copy it to a document, or bookmark the page.</li>
</ol>
