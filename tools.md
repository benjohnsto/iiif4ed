## Storytelling & Digital Narratives

### [Cogapp’s Exhibit](https://exhibit.so/)
Exhibit (from University of St Andrews): A highly accessible tool that allows users to create interactive presentations. Students can zoom into specific details of a IIIF manifest and add descriptive text. It’s excellent for "close reading" assignments.

### [Storiiies](https://storiiies.cogapp.com/)
Storiiies (by Cogapp): A simple editor for creating annotated tours. It’s very intuitive for students who might not have deep technical backgrounds but want to showcase research on specific image regions.

### [Annona]()
A set of web components that can create "storyboards" or "carousels" from IIIF annotations. It’s a great way to turn a list of student-made annotations into a cohesive visual essay.

## Collaborative Annotation & Transcription

### Liiive
An open-source, web-based tool designed specifically for real-time, collaborative exploration and annotation of IIIF images. Liiive allows you to create shared, in-person annotation assignments.

### [Recogito]()
(Pelagios Network): While transitioning through various versions, Recogito remains a powerhouse for semantic annotation. Students can tag places (linking to gazetteers), people, or events directly on the image.

### [FromThePage]()
If your assignment involves transcription, this tool uses IIIF to serve images to students while they type out the text. It’s perfect for a "crowdsourced" class project to transcribe a specific historical ledger or letter.

### [IIIF Curation Platform]()
A suite of tools from Japan (CODH) that allows for "image cropping" and "curating" specific parts of various manifests into a new, personal collection.

### [CroppingTool](https://mcgrawcenter.github.io/croppingtool/)
A freely available tool for cropping details from IIIF images. Croped details can be copied and pasted into disuccion boards or other communication tools.

## Digital Exhibits & Collections

### [Anaver.se](https://anaver.se/)
A IIIF-enabled online 3D digital exhibit environment.


## Visual Analysis

### [Mirador](https://projectmirador.org/)
A powerful, customizable viewer ideal for side-by-side comparisons and deep zooming.

### [Universal Viewer](https://universalviewer.io/)
Simple, clean interface for viewing single or multiple resources.

### [Tify]()
A slim, fast viewer that is excellent for looking at large collections or multi-page documents (like digitized books) where the user needs to quickly jump between pages.
